# Code of Conduct · 行为守则

## Our standard · 我们的标准

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and learning-focused community. 我们欢迎不同背景、语言、经验和观点的参与者，并以善意、具体、可验证的方式讨论代码与文化内容。

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized attention, trolling, deliberate disruption, and publishing private or copyrighted material without permission. 不得进行人身攻击、歧视、骚扰、威胁、曝光隐私、恶意刷屏，或把命理内容包装成恐吓、保证和替代专业意见。

## Enforcement · 执行

Please report concerns privately to the maintainer listed in `README.md`. Reports will be reviewed fairly and confidentially where possible. Maintainers may warn, edit, remove, or restrict participation when necessary. Serious or repeated violations may result in a permanent ban.

## Scope · 适用范围

This code applies to repository discussions, issues, pull requests, documentation, and project-related community spaces. 参与本项目即表示你同意遵守本守则及项目维护者为保护社区作出的合理决定。
